About PokéVault

Collectors selling to collectors, since the Base Set days.

Our story

PokéVault started as a shoebox of 1999 Base Set cards and a love of the hunt. Today we buy, sort, and resell vintage WotC-era holos and modern chase cards, from Neo Genesis Lugia to Evolving Skies alt arts.

We're a small team of collectors, so every card is personally handled: inspected under light and magnification, photographed, graded against the guide below, and packed by hand. If a card doesn't meet our description, we'll make it right.

At a glance

  • Vintage (1999–2003) and modern singles
  • Raw cards graded with a strict, published scale
  • Ships within 1–2 business days
  • Order requests confirmed by a real person

Condition & grading guide

How we describe raw (ungraded) cards. When in doubt, we grade down.

Mint Mint (M)

Pack-fresh. Perfect corners, edges, and surface with no visible wear even under close inspection. Typically well centered.

Near Mint Near Mint (NM)

Looks nearly unplayed. May have a tiny white speck on an edge or very light surface wear only visible under direct light.

Lightly Played Lightly Played (LP)

Minor edge or corner whitening and light scratches. No creases, bends, or major surface damage. Great for binders.

Moderately Played Moderately Played (MP)

Noticeable whitening, scuffing, and possibly a minor bend. Still fully intact and presentable.

Heavily Played Heavily Played (HP)

Heavy wear: significant whitening, scratches, and possibly small creases. Card is fully legible.

Damaged Damaged (DMG)

Major creases, tears, water damage, ink, or other serious defects. Priced as a filler or for nostalgia.

Shipping & authenticity

📦 Shipping

  • Every card ships in a penny sleeve, top-loader, and team bag.
  • Orders under $100: $4.99 tracked bubble mailer. Orders $100+: free tracked shipping.
  • Cards over $250 ship with signature confirmation and insurance.
  • Orders go out within 1–2 business days after payment.

🛡️ Authenticity guarantee

  • Each card passes a light test, texture check, and print/font comparison with known genuine copies.
  • We never sell proxies, reprints, or "custom" cards.
  • If a card is ever proven inauthentic, you get a full refund including shipping.
  • Need extra photos before buying? Use "Contact seller" on any listing.
